Accessibility

A room everyone can enter.

Statement date: July 27, 2026 · Grand Strategy Consulting LLC · hello@grandstrategy.llc

The Learning Circle exists so that people at different stages of work and life — professionals, creatives, students, elders — can learn together. That promise is empty if the front door is hard to open. This page explains what we do to keep this website and the programme accessible, what we know is not perfect yet, and how to tell us when something gets in your way.

This website

We aim toward the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2, Level AA. In practice, this site is built with:

  • semantic HTML with a logical heading structure and labeled landmarks;
  • menus and forms that work with a keyboard alone, with visible focus indicators throughout;
  • form fields with proper labels, clearly marked required fields and described errors;
  • text sized in relative units so it scales with your browser settings, and color never used as the only carrier of meaning;
  • no autoplaying media, no flashing content, no advertising or tracking scripts;
  • pages that remain readable and usable on small screens and at high zoom.

We do not claim perfect conformance — honesty over badges. If an automated checker and a human with a screen reader disagree, we side with the human.

The live programme

Circle sessions are live, online and designed to be participated in more than watched. We build in accessibility by default where we can — teaching and demonstration segments are recorded so you can review them at your own pace, and materials are provided digitally so they work with assistive technology.

Beyond the defaults, accommodations are individual. The application includes a dedicated question about access requirements, and it is there for a reason: tell us what would make full participation possible — live captions, materials in advance, movement breaks, alternative formats, or something we have not thought of — and we will work with you on it before the Circle begins. Asking for an accommodation never affects how an application is assessed.

Known limitations

Some parts of the experience run on third-party services we do not control — form processing, Stripe’s payment pages and email providers. We choose established providers that publish their own accessibility commitments, but we cannot fix their interfaces ourselves. If one of these blocks you, contact us and we will find another route: there is always a way to apply, enroll and participate that does not depend on a single vendor’s form.

Tell us what got in your way

If anything on this site or in the programme is difficult to use with your setup — screen reader, magnification, keyboard-only, captions, anything — email hello@grandstrategy.llc. Include the page and what happened if you can. We read every report, we aim to respond within a few business days, and fixing access barriers goes to the top of the list, not the bottom.
