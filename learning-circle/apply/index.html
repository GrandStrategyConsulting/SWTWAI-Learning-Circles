Before you apply

This Circle is designed for people who: have real professional, creative, business or community work to bring; want to make meaningful progress on an AI-supported project, idea or workflow; can attend the majority of eight live sessions; are willing to practise between sessions; value confidentiality, curiosity, intergenerational exchange and respectful disagreement; are willing to follow the Three Agreements: HUMBLE, KIND and WISE; and want to retain human responsibility for the work AI supports.

You do not need to be an expert. You do need to be willing to participate.
