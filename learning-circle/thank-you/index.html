What happens next

  1. Application review. We review the work you want to bring, your availability and what you hope to gain from the Circle.

  3. Acceptance and enrollment. If the programme is a good fit, you will receive an acceptance email and secure enrollment link.
  4. Diagnostic and welcome materials. After enrollment, you will complete the baseline diagnostic and receive the Practice Kit, the Three Agreements, confidentiality expectations and preparation guidance.
  5. The Circle begins. The first session is Saturday, September 5, 2026, at 10:00 a.m. Eastern.
