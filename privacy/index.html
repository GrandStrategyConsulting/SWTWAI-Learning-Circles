Privacy Policy

Your information, handled the way we teach.

Effective date: July 26, 2026 · Grand Strategy Consulting LLC, Maryland, United States · hello@grandstrategy.llc

We teach people to use AI with judgment, verification and respect for the humans behind the data. This policy holds us to the same standard. It is written in plain language on purpose: if anything here is unclear, that is our failure, not yours — email us and we will explain.

The principles behind this policy

Three commitments shape everything below. We collect the minimum. If we do not need a piece of information to serve you, we do not ask for it. A human stays in the lead. Decisions about you — including whether an application to the Learning Circle is a good fit — are made by a person, never delegated to an automated system or an AI model. Your data is never a verdict, and never a product. We do not sell it, rent it, trade it, or use it to build advertising profiles. Not now, not ever.

What we collect, and why

If you browse this site: almost nothing. This is a static website. We do not set advertising or tracking cookies. Our hosting providers keep standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type, and pages requested) for security and reliability, as virtually all web hosting does.

If you apply to the Learning Circle: the information you type into the application — your name, contact details, work background, the project you want to move forward, your current AI use and concerns, your availability, and anything else you choose to share. We ask for it for exactly one purpose: to understand your goals, prepare for your discovery call, and assess whether the Circle can genuinely serve you. Optional fields are truly optional.

If you join the future-cohort list: your name, email, and whatever you optionally share about your interest. We use it for exactly one thing: telling you when the next cohort opens.

If you enroll: what we need to run the program — your enrollment details, session participation, workflow submissions you choose to share, and your program diagnostics (baseline, midpoint, final, and 30-day). Payment is different: payments are processed by Stripe on their secure systems. Your card number never touches our website or our hands.

If you email us: your message and address, so we can reply.

How we use AI with your information

We run our business on AI-supported workflows — it is what we teach — so you deserve to know exactly where the line sits. AI tools may help us draft communications and organize our work, always with human review before anything reaches you. We do not enter your confidential or sensitive personal information into public AI tools — the same rule we teach every participant. And no AI system makes a decision about you: acceptance into the Circle, feedback on your work, and every reply you receive carry a human’s name and a human’s judgment behind them. Model predicts; human verifies.

Confidentiality inside the Circle

The Learning Circle works because people can bring real work and unfinished thinking into the room. We protect that structurally: peer-sharing segments of sessions are never recorded; teaching and demonstration recordings are available only to your cohort, during the program and for 90 days afterward; and participants’ work examples stay in the room under the cohort’s confidentiality expectations, which every participant — and every facilitator and guest — agrees to.

Case studies and results

Program results become public case studies only in anonymized form, and only according to the consent choices you make during onboarding — not the fact of having applied, and not by default. You can change or withdraw that consent at any time by emailing us, and withdrawal applies to all future use. We never invent testimonials, and we never publish your words or results without your written permission.

Who else touches your data

A small number of service providers process data on our behalf, under their own security and privacy obligations: our form processor (application and waitlist submissions), Stripe (payments), our email provider (correspondence and program communications), and our hosting providers (this website). We share your information with no one else, with one exception: if the law genuinely requires it, in which case we will tell you unless we are legally prevented from doing so.

How long we keep things

Applications that do not lead to enrollment are deleted within twelve months of the cohort’s close, unless you ask us to keep you in mind for future cohorts. Waitlist details are kept until you unsubscribe or the list is retired. Participant records are kept for the program plus the period needed for our legitimate business and legal obligations, after which they are deleted or anonymized. Anonymized measurement data — from which you cannot be identified — may be retained to evidence the program’s effectiveness.

Your rights — wherever you live

Depending on where you live, laws such as the GDPR, UK GDPR, or U.S. state privacy laws give you rights over your data: to see it, correct it, delete it, restrict its use, receive a copy, or withdraw consent. Our position is simpler than the legal map: we honor these requests from everyone, regardless of jurisdiction. Email hello@grandstrategy.llc and we will respond within 30 days. You will never be treated worse for exercising a right.

Children

The Circle is proudly intergenerational, but this site and program are directed at adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 16. Participants under 18 may only enroll with the involvement and consent of a parent or guardian.

Security, honestly stated

We use reputable providers, access controls, and the data-minimization habit described above — the less we hold, the less can go wrong. No one can honestly promise perfect security, so we will not. What we promise instead: if a breach ever affects your personal information, we will tell you promptly, plainly, and completely, and we will tell you what we are doing about it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in any way that matters, we will update the effective date above and, for participants and list members, tell you directly by email. We will never use a quiet policy update to do something with your data that this version promised we would not.
